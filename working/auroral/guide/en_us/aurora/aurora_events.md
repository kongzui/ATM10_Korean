# <Color id="gold">Aurora Events</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="aurora_shard" scale="2" />

  The celestial phenomenon at the heart of Auroral. When the lights come out, the cold biomes wake up.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">When Auroras Occur</Color>
</Column>

Auroras have a **33% chance** to begin each night in cold biomes. While one is active:

* The sky fills with green and pink ribbons of light.
* The night is never fully dark.
* The mod's magical effects switch on.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Effects</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="glacial_basin" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Liquid Aura</Color>
</Row>

<ItemLink id="glacial_basin" />s exposed to the sky slowly fill with Liquid Aura, the magical substance needed for Shimmersteel infusion.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="shimmersteel_pickaxe" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Gear Repair</Color>
</Row>

Shimmersteel tools and Shimmerweave armor repair themselves while equipped during an aurora.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="auroral_nautilus_spawn_egg" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Creature Spawning</Color>
</Row>

Auroral Nautiluses drift in near players during aurora events.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="frozen_petals" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Flora Growth</Color>
</Row>

<ItemLink id="aurora_bloom" />s appear on snow blocks, ready to be harvested for Frozen Petals.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Aurora Catalyst</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hearthwood_log" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Hearthwood Log</Color>
</Row>

A lit <ItemLink id="hearthwood_log" /> within 32 blocks of a player in a cold biome adds **+15% aurora chance** at nightfall. Up to three logs stack for **+45%**, pushing the nightly chance to about **78%** on default settings.

The catalyst is a strong boost, not a guarantee. For near-certain auroras, keep several lit logs near where you sleep and make sure you are in a cold biome when night begins.

<RecipeFor id="hearthwood_log" />

A lit Hearthwood Log also grants Frostbite Immunity, repels Phantoms, and improves villager trades.
