<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">The Warning</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="auroral_nautilus_spawn_egg" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">The Nautili Take Note</Color>
</Row>

Eating a Roasted Snowball deeply offends the Auroral Nautili. Once you commit the act, wild Nautili **will no longer spawn near you** during auroras. The skies grow quiet. The chimes fade.

You'll see the message *"The Magestic Nautili will no longer Soar In your skies"* — and they mean it.
