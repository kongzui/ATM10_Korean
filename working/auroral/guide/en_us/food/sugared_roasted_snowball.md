<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Properties</Color>
</Column>

* **Minor Nutrition** — same small bite as the base Roasted Snowball
* **Always Edible** — can be eaten at full hunger
* **Apology Flavoured** — eating one makes peace with the Auroral Nautili

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">The Apology</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="auroral_nautilus_spawn_egg" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">The Nautili Forgive</Color>
</Row>

If you've eaten a <ItemLink id="roasted_snowball" />, the Nautili have flown off in disgust. Eating a Sugared Roasted Snowball tells them you're sorry, and they return on the next aurora.

You'll see *"The Majestic Nautili fly once again!"* confirming your good standing has been restored.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Tips</Color>
</Column>

* Craft one before you ever eat the first Roasted Snowball — you may regret it otherwise.
* The glint distinguishes it at a glance from its plain sibling.
* No effect if the Nautili already favor you, but it still tastes sweet.
