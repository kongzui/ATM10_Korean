# <Color id="gold">Shimmersteel Armor Trims</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="shimmersteel_ingot" scale="2" />

  Use a Shimmersteel Ingot as an armor trim material to give any armor an icy blue accent reminiscent of the aurora itself.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Applying the Trim</Color>
</Column>

1. Obtain a **Smithing Template** (any armor trim pattern from vanilla structures).
2. Place the template, your armor piece, and a <ItemLink id="shimmersteel_ingot" /> in a Smithing Table.
3. The resulting armor takes on an ice-blue trim that shimmers between blue and silver.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Appearance</Color>
</Column>

The Shimmersteel trim has a distinctive icy gradient:

* Deep blue shadows
* Cool steel-blue midtones
* Bright ice-white highlights

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Compatibility</Color>
</Column>

Shimmersteel trims work with:

* All vanilla armor types — leather, chainmail, iron, gold, diamond, netherite
* Most modded armor that supports the vanilla trim system
* All vanilla trim patterns — Coast, Dune, Eye, Host, Raiser, Rib, Sentry, Shaper, Silence, Snout, Spire, Tide, Vex, Ward, Wayfinder, Wild

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Tips</Color>
</Column>

* Pairs especially well with diamond and iron armor for a cohesive cold theme.
* Combine with Shimmerweave armor for the full frozen-adventurer look.
* The high enchantability of Shimmersteel means trimmed armor still enchants beautifully.
