<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Step 2 — Glacial Basin Infusion</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="glacial_basin" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Infusion</Color>
</Row>

Infuse the Unrefined Shimmersteel in a <ItemLink id="glacial_basin" /> filled with Liquid Aura.

1. Place a Glacial Basin under open sky in a cold biome.
2. Wait for an aurora to fill the basin completely (3/3 aura).
3. Right-click the basin with Unrefined Shimmersteel.
4. The basin consumes **all** its Liquid Aura to produce one Shimmersteel Ingot.

**Important:** the basin must be completely full to perform this infusion.
