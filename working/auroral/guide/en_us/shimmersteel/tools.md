<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>

</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="shimmersteel_sword" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Slow, Execute, Snow Mark</Color>
</Row>

* **Slowness I** — every hit applies it to the target.
* **Execute** — deals massive bonus damage to enemies below 15% health.
* **Snow Mark** — places a snow layer where executed enemies fall.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Hoe</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="shimmersteel_hoe" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Silk Touch &amp; Shimmer Soil Creation</Color>
</Row>

* **Inherent Silk Touch** — harvests crops and blocks as if Silk Touch were applied.
* **Shimmer Soil Creation** — right-click snow blocks, powder snow, dirt, grass, or snow layers to make <ItemLink id="shimmer_soil" />.
* **Farmland Upgrade** — right-click regular farmland to upgrade it (moisture preserved).
* Perfect for starting farms in frozen biomes without needing dirt.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="shimmer_soil" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Shimmer Soil</Color>
</Row>

This magical farmland accelerates crop growth:

* **3x faster** at night
* **5x faster** during auroras
* Emits a faint glow (light level 2)
* Hydrated by water **or** <ItemLink id="shimmering_ice" /> within 4 blocks
* Won't revert to dirt while crops are planted
* Accepts any vanilla or modded crop seed, just like regular farmland

Pair with Shimmering Ice for glowing underground farms that never need water.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
