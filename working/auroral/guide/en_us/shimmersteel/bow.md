<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Star-Shots</Color>
</Column>

The Shimmersteel Bow uses **snowballs** as ammunition. Each shot transforms the snowball into a Star-Shot.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="shimmersteel_bow" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Properties</Color>
</Row>

* **Damage** — higher base damage than arrows
* **Flashbang** — enemies near the impact are briefly blinded and disoriented
* **Light Trail** — visible particle trail through the air
* **No Pickup** — Star-Shots cannot be recovered after firing

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Tips</Color>
</Column>

* Snowballs are plentiful in cold biomes — ammo is rarely a worry.
* The flashbang is excellent for crowd control around dense groups.
* Repairs itself during auroras, like all Shimmersteel gear.
