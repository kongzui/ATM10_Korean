# <Color id="gold">Glacial Basin</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="glacial_basin" scale="2" />

  Your primary workstation. Collects Liquid Aura during auroras and uses it to transform raw materials into their magical counterparts.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<RecipeFor id="glacial_basin" />

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">How It Works</Color>
</Column>

1. **Place** the basin under open sky.
2. **Wait** for an aurora to begin.
3. **Watch** the basin fill with glowing Liquid Aura (up to 3 levels).
4. **Right-click** with the input item to transform it.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Transformations</Color>
</Column>

| Input | Output | Aura Cost |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| <ItemLink id="unrefined_shimmersteel" /> | <ItemLink id="shimmersteel_ingot" /> | 3 (full) |
| <ItemLink id="woven_leather" /> | <ItemLink id="shimmerweave_fabric" /> | 1 |
| <ItemLink id="frozen_petals" /> | <ItemLink id="aurora_shard" /> | 1 |
| Ice | <ItemLink id="shimmering_ice" /> | 1 |

A Shimmersteel Ingot infusion requires the basin to be **completely full** and consumes all 3 levels of Liquid Aura at once.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Tips</Color>
</Column>

* Keep multiple basins placed to collect more aura per event.
* The basin must have a clear view of the sky.
* Auroras last the whole night, so there's plenty of time to fill.
