<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">How It Works</Color>
</Column>

The Cold Brewing Stand behaves exactly like the vanilla brewing stand, with one key difference: **snowballs** are the fuel.

Ideal for:

* Skyblock or peaceful playthroughs
* Players who haven't reached the Nether yet
* Cold-biome bases where snowballs are plentiful

  ## <Color id="gold">Frozen Petals as Nether Wart</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="frozen_petals" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Brewing the Base Potion</Color>
</Row>

<ItemLink id="frozen_petals" /> brew an **Awkward Potion** from a Water Bottle, exactly like Nether Wart. The full brewing tree opens without ever stepping into the Nether.

Steps:

1. Fill the stand with snowballs (up to 20).
2. Place Water Bottles in the bottom slots.
3. Add Frozen Petals to the ingredient slot.
4. Wait for brewing to complete — you'll have Awkward Potions.

From there, brew the rest of the potion tree with standard ingredients.
