<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Glacial Basin Infusion</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="glacial_basin" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Leather or Wool → Fabric</Color>
</Row>

Infuse Leather or any color of Wool in a <ItemLink id="glacial_basin" /> filled with Liquid Aura.

1. Place a Glacial Basin under open sky.
2. Wait for an aurora to fill it.
3. Right-click the basin with Leather or Wool.
4. Receive Shimmerweave Fabric.

  ## <Color id="gold">Properties</Color>
</Column>

* **Lightweight** — armor made from Shimmerweave has minimal weight
* **Aurora Affinity** — armor repairs itself during auroras
* **Magical Conductivity** — enables the special ability on each piece

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Uses</Color>
</Column>

Shimmerweave Fabric upgrades iron armor at the Smithing Table. You'll need a **Shimmersteel Upgrade Smithing Template** to perform the upgrade.

* <ItemImage id="shimmerweave_goggles" scale="0.75"/> [Shimmerweave Goggles](goggles.md) — reveals hidden enemies
* <ItemImage id="shimmerweave_tunic" scale="0.75"/> [Shimmerweave Tunic](tunic.md) — fire protection
* <ItemImage id="shimmerweave_leggings" scale="0.75"/> [Shimmerweave Leggings](leggings.md) — speed on snow
* <ItemImage id="shimmerweave_skates" scale="0.75"/> [Shimmerweave Skates](skates.md) — master of ice
