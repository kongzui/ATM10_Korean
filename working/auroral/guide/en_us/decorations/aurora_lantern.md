  ## <Color id="gold">Properties</Color>
</Column>

* **Light Level 15** — maximum brightness
* **Color Shifting** — gently cycles through aurora hues (green, blue, pink)
* **Placeable** — fits on floors, walls, or hung from ceilings
* **Waterproof** — works underwater without going out

  ### <Color id="aqua">Held In-Hand</Color>

While held in your main hand or off-hand, the Aurora Lantern grants immunity to the Darkness effect. Its ethereal glow dispels magical darkness, making it invaluable when exploring Deep Dark biomes or fighting the Warden.
