# <Color id="gold">Snow Angels</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="snow_angel" scale="2" />

  A playful imprint left in fresh snow. Temporary by default — preserve it with a Frozen Petal to keep it forever.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Creating a Snow Angel</Color>
</Column>

**Sneak and right-click** on any snow surface with **both hands empty**. Your character leaves an imprint in the snow, creating a decorative snow angel pattern.

A snow angel is marked as a *replaceable* block — you can place a new block directly into its space without breaking it first, the same way you would over tall grass or a snow layer.

Valid surfaces:

* Snow layers
* Snow blocks
* Powder snow

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Fading Away</Color>
</Column>

Snow angels are temporary by nature. Like footprints in fresh snow, they fade away after **5 minutes** if left alone.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Preserving</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="frozen_petals" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Right-Click with a Frozen Petal</Color>
</Row>

Right-click a snow angel with a <ItemLink id="frozen_petals" />. The petal's magic crystallizes the snow angel, stopping it from fading. Preserved snow angels remain until broken.

Frozen Petals come from harvesting fully grown <ItemLink id="aurora_bloom" />s during an aurora.
