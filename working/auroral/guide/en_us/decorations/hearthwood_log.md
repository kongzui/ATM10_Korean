# <Color id="gold">Hearthwood Log</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="hearthwood_log" scale="2" />

  A magical log that burns with an enchanted flame. A lit Hearthwood holds back the Frostbite, repels Phantoms, and tilts the night toward an aurora.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Obtaining</Color>
</Column>

Hearthwood Logs grow naturally in cold biomes and turn up in:

* Snowy Taiga villages
* Frozen structures
* Igloo basements (occasionally)

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Lighting It</Color>
</Column>

Right-click the log with **Flint and Steel** to ignite it. Once lit, it burns with a warm, magical glow and starts giving off its passive effects. A burnt-out log can be relit the same way.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Effects</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hearthwood_log" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Extended Burn</Color>
</Row>

A lit Hearthwood Log burns for **7 in-game days** of chunk-loaded time (168,000 ticks, roughly 2 hours 20 minutes of continuous play). The timer only ticks down while the chunk is active, so logs in rarely-visited chunks last much longer in real time. Burn time is configurable in the server config.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hearthwood_log" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Frostbite Immunity</Color>
</Row>

Players within a **16-block radius** of a lit log are immune to Frostbite. Make a ring of them and your base stays safe through the worst of winter.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hearthwood_log" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Phantom Ward</Color>
</Row>

Any Phantom that strays within 16 blocks of a lit log catches fire on contact. Phantoms also refuse to target players currently warmed by Frostbite Immunity, so a ring of lit Hearthwoods keeps the night skies clear.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hearthwood_log" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Aurora Catalyst</Color>
</Row>

A lit log within **32 blocks** of a player in a cold biome adds **+15% aurora chance** at nightfall. Up to three logs stack for **+45%**, pushing the nightly aurora chance to about 78% on default settings. Logs beyond the third do not stack.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="hearthwood_log" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Villager Comfort</Color>
</Row>

Villagers near a lit log feel more at ease and may offer better trades.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Tips</Color>
</Column>

* Place logs around your cold-biome base for permanent Frostbite protection.
* The warm glow doubles as ambient lighting.
* Three lit logs is the sweet spot for the aurora bonus; the fourth does nothing extra.
