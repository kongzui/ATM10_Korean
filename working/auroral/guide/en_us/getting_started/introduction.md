# <Color id="gold">Introduction to Auroral</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="frozen_petals" scale="2" />

  Auroral turns cold biomes from barren wastes into hubs of late-game progression. Harness the Aurora Borealis to craft magical items, enhanced tools, and powerful armor.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">The Aurora</Color>
</Column>

A celestial event that occurs randomly during nights in cold biomes. When the aurora is active, the sky fills with shimmering ribbons of green and pink light. This is when the magic happens.

While an aurora is active:

* **Liquid Aura** can be collected in <ItemLink id="glacial_basin" />s
* **Shimmersteel gear** repairs itself automatically
* **Auroral Nautiluses** may spawn in the skies
* **Aurora Blooms** appear on snow blocks

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Cold Biomes</Color>
</Column>

Auroral's features are designed for cold biomes:

* Snowy Plains
* Ice Spikes
* Frozen Ocean
* Frozen Peaks
* Grove
* Snowy Taiga

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Progression Path</Color>
</Column>

1. Wait for an aurora in a cold biome.
2. Harvest <ItemLink id="frozen_petals" /> from naturally spawning Aurora Blooms.
3. Craft a <ItemLink id="glacial_basin" /> and a <ItemLink id="cold_brewing_stand" />.
4. Collect Liquid Aura during auroras to infuse iron into <ItemLink id="shimmersteel_ingot" />.
5. Craft enhanced tools, weapons, and armor.
