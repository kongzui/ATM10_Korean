<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>

***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Gather Frozen Petals</Color>
</Column>

<ItemLink id="frozen_petals" /> are the key to unlocking the rest of the mod. Sources:

* Harvested from <ItemLink id="aurora_bloom" />s that spawn naturally on snow during auroras.
* Dropped by Auroral Nautiluses during auroras.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Craft the Glacial Basin</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="glacial_basin" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Glacial Basin</Color>
</Row>

The Glacial Basin is your primary workstation. Place it outside during an aurora to fill it with Liquid Aura.

<RecipeFor id="glacial_basin" />

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Your First Shimmersteel</Color>
</Column>

Once your Glacial Basin has collected Liquid Aura:

1. Right-click the basin while holding an Iron Ingot.
2. The iron transforms into <ItemLink id="shimmersteel_ingot" />.
3. Use Shimmersteel to craft enhanced tools and weapons.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

  ### <Color id="aqua">Awkward Potions Without Nether Wart</Color>

Frozen Petals brew an Awkward Potion from a Water Bottle, exactly like Nether Wart. The whole brewing tree opens up without ever stepping into the Nether.
