<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="shimmering_ice" scale="2" />

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Properties</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="shimmering_ice" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Never Melts</Color>
</Row>

Completely resistant to heat and light. Perfect for decorative builds and functional paths that need to last.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="shimmering_ice" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Light Emission</Color>
</Row>

Emits a soft glow at light level 8 — enough to prevent mob spawning while keeping the ambiance.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="shimmering_ice" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Hydration</Color>
</Row>

Hydrates vanilla farmland and <ItemLink id="shimmer_soil" /> within a **4-block radius**, just like water. Combined with the soft glow, this makes it excellent for underground farms with no water channels.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="glow_leek" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Glow Leek Substrate</Color>
</Row>

<ItemLink id="glow_leek_seeds" /> can only be planted on Shimmering Ice — its magical properties are essential for growing the bioluminescent crop.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Shimmer Soil Synergy</Color>
</Column>

Pair Shimmering Ice with <ItemLink id="shimmer_soil" /> (created with a Shimmersteel Hoe) for the ultimate frozen-biome farm:

* Shimmering Ice provides hydration **and** light
* Shimmer Soil grows crops faster — **3x at night, 5x during an aurora**
* No water needed — works anywhere
