<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Growing</Color>
</Column>

Glow Leeks grow only on <ItemLink id="shimmering_ice" /> blocks. They do not require light and continue growing through the night.

When fully grown, breaking a plant drops:

* **Glow Leek** (the vegetable)
* **Glow Leek Seeds** (for replanting)

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Eating</Color>
</Column>

Eating a Glow Leek grants:

* **Night Vision** — see clearly in the dark
* **Glowing** — you become visible to others
